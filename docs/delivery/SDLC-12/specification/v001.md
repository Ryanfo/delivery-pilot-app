# Specification: Rename the page heading to "My tasks"

> Provenance (ticket, revision, run, inputs) is added by the coordinator on publication.

## Problem and outcome
The single page of the app shows the main heading "Task list" (`src/App.tsx:46`, `<h1>Task list</h1>`).
The product owner wants the heading to read "My tasks". When this is done, anyone opening the
app sees "My tasks" as the main heading. Nothing else on the page changes.

## Scope
- In scope: the text of the main page heading (the only `<h1>` in the app, currently in
  `src/App.tsx`), changing it from "Task list" to "My tasks".
- In scope: updating the existing automated tests that check the heading text so they check
  the new text. Currently `e2e/app.spec.ts:5` asserts a heading named "Task list". No unit or
  component test checks the heading today.

## Exclusions
- Out of scope: the browser tab title (`index.html` `<title>Task list</title>` stays as it is).
- Out of scope: styling of the heading (element, level, classes, CSS).
- Out of scope: any other wording on the page, including the "HELLO WORLD" welcome block,
  the "Status" and "Starred only" filter labels, the "Showing X of Y tasks · N starred" count
  line and the task list itself (accessible name "Tasks").
- Out of scope: repository and documentation titles that use "Task list" (`README.md`,
  `CLAUDE.md` "Task list pilot app") and historical delivery records under `docs/delivery/`.

## Acceptance criteria
- **AC1**: Given the app is loaded, when the page renders, then the main heading at the top of
  the page (heading level 1) reads exactly "My tasks".
- **AC2**: Given the app is loaded, when the page renders, then no page heading has the text
  "Task list".
- **AC3**: Given the app is loaded, when the page renders, then everything else on the page is
  unchanged from current behaviour: the "HELLO WORLD" welcome block (region "Welcome"), the
  "Status" and "Starred only" filters and how they filter, the "Showing X of Y tasks · N
  starred" count line, and the task list with its star toggles and persistence.

## Non-functional requirements
- Accessibility: the heading stays a level-1 heading, so assistive technology finds it by
  role "heading" with the accessible name "My tasks". The page keeps a single `<h1>`.
- Performance, security, privacy, compatibility: no impact expected.

## Constraints
- Follow `docs/testing-policy.md`: each acceptance criterion maps to at least one named test.
  Update existing tests to assert the new text; do not weaken, skip or delete them.
- Follow `docs/guardrails.md`: no new runtime dependencies. Do not change `CLAUDE.md`,
  `.github/`, `.claude/` or `docs/delivery/`.
- The coordinator runs these checks: lint, typecheck, unit, build, e2e.

## Designs and attachments
None.

## Dependencies
- Existing page layout in `src/App.tsx`: heading, welcome block (SDLC-7), filters including
  "Starred only" (SDLC-10), count line and task list.
- No other in-flight tickets are listed in `related_work`.

## Assumptions
- A1: "My tasks" is the exact text, with a lowercase "t" and no trailing punctuation, as
  written in the brief.
- A2: The "main heading at the top of the page" is the existing `<h1>` in `src/App.tsx`. The
  app has no other headings.
- A3: The tab title in `index.html` keeps reading "Task list". The brief puts it out of scope,
  so the tab title and the heading will differ after this change.
